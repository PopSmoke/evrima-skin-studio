GET STARTED
Extract the ZIP into its own folder and open Skin Studio using the launcher.
Design: choose your dinosaur, pattern and colors. The preview follows your edits.
View: adjust the preview lighting and detail.
Tools: find experiments, comparisons, your library and recovery copies.
Help & About: read plain-English tips, known color interactions and credits.

SAVE YOUR WORK
Save project keeps the colors and full setup, including the dinosaur and pattern.
Export skin creates a JSON file named after your design. Skin exports do not
select a dinosaur or pattern in game. Keep a project too if you want to return
to the full setup. Save image exports a picture of the preview.
Save libraries and checkpoint sets separately to keep them after closing.
Recovery copies are optional and must be enabled each session.

TRY PATTERNS TOGETHER
Leave Audition patterns open while you edit. Its cards refresh after a short
pause. Choose a card and Apply to use that pattern; the window stays open.

ABOUT THE PREVIEW
In-game lighting and patterns may change the result. Bright colors do not
automatically mean glow. This edition includes one idle animation per dinosaur.
Game models and textures belong to their respective owners. Skin Studio is
an independent tool, not an official product of The Isle.

More detail: Help & About in Studio, TECHNICAL NOTES.txt and CREDITS.txt.

COMPACT TEXTURE STORAGE
Textures and palette pictures use lossless compression. No texture resolution,
color precision, dinosaur model, pattern or adult idle has been removed.

VERSION 1.4.1
Corrected feather materials for Austroraptor, Hypsilophodon and Oviraptor.
Psittacosaurus now uses its original painted textures and masked bristles.
Its body color values are preserved in designs but do not recolor this legacy material.
The launcher keeps its console open while Studio runs so startup errors remain visible.
Keep that console open until you close Studio.
